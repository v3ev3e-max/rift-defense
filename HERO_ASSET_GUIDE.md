# 3V_TD Hero Asset Guide — Phase 5

## Current cast
- Female (7): Yuria, Reina, Arin, Karin, Sera, Luna, Mia
- Male (3): Noel, Ian, Leon

## Asset paths
- Battle chibi: `public/assets/heroes/<id>.png` — 160x160 transparent/faded PNG
- Archive portrait: `public/assets/portraits/<id>.jpg` — 512x768 JPG

## Runtime behavior
- Phaser combat loads the chibi PNGs.
- Hero archive/deck cards load the larger portraits.
- Legacy save IDs `eve` and `lize` are migrated automatically to `ian` and `leon`.
